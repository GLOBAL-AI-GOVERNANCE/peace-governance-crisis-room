# Release Lineage

## Public browser line

| Release | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `v0.3.0-rc2` | Primer closure and contract hardening | Published public prerelease; exact release-commit deployed UAT passed |
| `v0.3.0-rc1` | Integrated web review candidate | Historical public prerelease |
| `v0.2.2` | Source-readiness baseline under the historical title | Superseded; do not use for current training or assessment |
| `v0.2.1` | Earlier public candidate with later-identified scoring defects | Superseded; do not use for training, assessment, or comparative scoring |

## Internal and experimental lines

Historical internal RC3/RC4 packages are evidence archives, not later public versions and not merge bases. The Godot client remains experimental and does not inherit browser validation. Windows packaging remains a separate release path.

## Product boundary

Peace OS is the product and doctrine name. It is not a computer operating system, operational decision authority, intelligence product, media-authentication service, emergency-response platform, legal-attribution engine, certification product, or autonomous release authority.
