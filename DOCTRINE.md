# Peace OS Doctrine

## Core Doctrine

Peace OS: Crisis Room is built around one governing principle:

> Do not let AI accelerate crisis before truth catches up.

The project turns AI-governance doctrine into a playable decision system. It teaches that the safest response to crisis information is not silence, panic, blame, or automatic release. The safest response is disciplined verification under pressure.

## Operating Covenant

The doctrine is expressed through four public rules:

1. **Verification before amplification.**
2. **Human control before release.**
3. **Civilian protection before intelligence value.**
4. **Confidence scoring before public attribution.**

A fifth rule governs all future development:

5. **AI may advise. AI may not decide.**

## The Decision Chain

Every scenario should force the player through this chain:

```text
Signal
→ Source
→ Evidence
→ Confidence
→ Civilian Risk
→ Release Language
→ Governance Action
→ Consequence
→ After-Action Review
```

The player should not be rewarded for being first. The player should be rewarded for being accurate, bounded, proportionate, protective, and reviewable.

## What the Player Learns

The game teaches decision discipline in crisis-information environments where viral media, synthetic content, public pressure, fragmented evidence, and institutional risk collide.

Players practice how to:

- distinguish observation from inference,
- identify missing metadata and chain-of-custody gaps,
- avoid attribution beyond available evidence,
- protect civilians and vulnerable communities,
- recognize bot amplification and manipulated media risk,
- select controlled public language,
- preserve human review gates,
- document why a decision was made.

## Governance Meters

The game measures decisions through six meters:

| Meter | Purpose | Good Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Truth Integrity | Did the player match claims to evidence? | Higher |
| Escalation Risk | Did decisions inflame or slow crisis pressure? | Lower |
| Civilian Safety | Were vulnerable people protected? | Higher |
| Institutional Credibility | Would the process survive scrutiny later? | Higher |
| Decision Timeliness | Did the player act fast enough without overclaiming? | Higher |
| Public Pressure | How intense is demand for immediate public language? | Lower |

## Safe Release Standard

Public language should never outrun confidence. When confidence is low, the safest language should make clear that:

- reports are circulating,
- review is ongoing,
- media has not been authenticated,
- translations or context may be uncertain,
- civilian protection remains active,
- no legal or operational attribution has been made.

## Scenario Design Rules

Every scenario should be:

- fictional or sanitized,
- bounded to education and training,
- free of classified or operational data,
- free of real civilian identities,
- designed around uncertainty rather than target identification,
- scored for governance discipline rather than blame assignment.

A valid Peace OS scenario should include:

- at least one incomplete or ambiguous source,
- at least one public-pressure element,
- at least one civilian-protection concern,
- at least one information-integrity risk,
- at least one tempting overclaim path,
- at least one safe controlled-language path,
- an after-action review explaining the decision.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

This project must not become:

- an operational intelligence system,
- a legal attribution engine,
- a targeting tool,
- a propaganda system,
- a live crisis-response platform,
- a replacement for expert review,
- an autonomous escalation system.

No live AI, live incident ingestion, classified information, real witness identity data, operational tasking, or automated attribution is included in v0.2.1.

## The Trusted Node Standard

Peace OS is the trusted-node standard between signal, system, decision, and truth.

A trusted node does five things:

1. Identifies the weak link.
2. Grades the evidence.
3. Assigns the owner.
4. Bounds the next action.
5. Prevents premature escalation.

## Development Doctrine

Future versions should preserve the same progression:

```text
Doctrine first.
Safety boundary second.
Scenario data third.
Game mechanic fourth.
Runtime test fifth.
Public release last.
```

No v0.3 work should begin until v0.2.1 has passed the physical gate:

```text
Run it in Godot.
Export Windows.
Test on an external Windows PC.
Package the Windows ZIP.
Publish the executable as a separate release asset.
```

## Final Doctrine Statement

Peace OS: Crisis Room exists to make verification playable.

Its purpose is to train humans to slow down the dangerous moment when machines, media, institutions, and public pressure begin converting uncertainty into escalation.
