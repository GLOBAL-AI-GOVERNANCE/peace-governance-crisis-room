# Doctrine

Peace OS: Crisis Room converts a small set of principles into testable simulation gates.

1. Verify before amplifying.
2. Separate observation, interpretation, possibility, and confirmation.
3. Preserve human authority before any simulated release.
4. Protect civilians and sensitive identities before informational advantage.
5. Communicate uncertainty honestly.
6. Use bounded, reversible, reviewable actions.
7. Treat pressure as context, not permission to overclaim.
8. AI may advise. AI may not decide.

Peace OS is the product name, not a computer operating system. The simulation is fictional. Its metrics are authored educational constructs, not scientific predictions of crisis behavior.
