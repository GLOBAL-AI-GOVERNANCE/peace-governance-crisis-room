# Security Policy

## Do Not Upload Sensitive Data

Do not upload or commit:

- classified information
- operational incident records
- live maritime data
- personally identifying civilian data
- witness names or locations
- real evidence from active incidents
- restricted government or partner material

## Intended Data Type

This repository is for fictional, scripted, sanitized, historical, or publicly releasable training scenarios only.

## AI Boundary

This game includes no live AI integration. AI may be represented as a fictional advisory concept inside scenarios, but AI does not decide, attribute, escalate, or authorize any action.
