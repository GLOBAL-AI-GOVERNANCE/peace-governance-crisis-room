# Human Learning Validation Plan

Minimum preliminary pilot:

- 6 to 10 participants with no prior exposure;
- fixed facilitator script;
- consent and privacy-appropriate observation;
- pre/post instrument covering verification, uncertainty, civilian protection, controlled language, human authority, correction, and de-escalation;
- task completion and misconception log;
- pass threshold declared before testing.

Provisional source-team threshold: at least 80% of participants improve or maintain correct responses on every critical doctrine item, no participant leaves with a newly introduced dangerous misconception, and all critical UX defects are remediated. Human-subject and institutional requirements must be determined before formal research claims.
