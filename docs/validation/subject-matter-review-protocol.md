# Subject-Matter Review Protocol

Before workshop use, at least one independent practitioner with relevant crisis communications, OSINT verification, civilian-protection, maritime, humanitarian, or information-integrity experience should review:

- scenario realism and missing alternatives;
- evidence labels and expected markings;
- confidence ranges;
- controlled public language;
- recommended actions and point weights;
- possible cultural, geopolitical, or escalation bias;
- risks of teaching false certainty.

Record reviewer qualifications, conflicts, findings, changes accepted or rejected, rationale, and residual limitations. This is expert review, not regulatory approval.
